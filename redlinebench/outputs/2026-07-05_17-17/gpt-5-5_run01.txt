## Project context

- **Project:** Bonfire House, unaddressed property in Chatham County, North Carolina; jurisdiction listed as Chatham County Building Inspectors/Central Permitting; code basis listed as **2018 North Carolina Residential Code** adopting IRC 2015 with amendments.
- **Building:** New single-family residence, R-3 occupancy, Type V-B construction as stated; two stories plus crawlspace/garage, pool/deck/site work.
- **Climate zone:** 4A, stated in code summary.
- **Drawing scope:** General/architectural sheets only: code summary, site/floor/roof plans, exterior/interior elevations, building/wall/deck sections, assemblies, details, RCP, schedules; structural, MEP, civil, pool, and detailed site engineering are not included.
- **Specifications:** 11-page narrative material/application/allowance booklet, not organized as formal CSI divisions; includes site, foundation, framing, envelope, interiors, HVAC/plumbing/electrical fixtures, pool, septic/well, utilities, and allowances/TBD selections.

## Summary

The set communicates a clear design intent and includes many useful architectural plans, elevations, assemblies, and interior elevations, but it is not yet tightly coordinated for bidding or construction. The most serious concerns are building-envelope/waterproofing gaps, especially the low-slope metal roofs, shower assemblies, crawlspace, and exterior drainage. The second major concern is code/life-safety documentation, particularly garage separation, emergency escape openings, safety glazing, pool barriers, guards, handrails, and smoke/CO detection. The third major concern is drawing/specification coordination, with multiple product conflicts, TBD selections, missing hardware/fixture information, and deferred pool/solar/electrical/site details likely to generate RFIs and change orders.
