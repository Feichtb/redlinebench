- **Code/type:** 2018 North Carolina Residential Code, based on the 2015 IRC with amendments; two-story single-family residence, identified as R-3, Type V-B.

## Summary

The set contains substantial architectural detail, but significant inconsistencies and unresolved interfaces remain for bidding and construction. The three greatest concerns are envelope waterproofing and roof-system definition, garage/pool/stair life-safety coordination, and conflicting material, dimensional, and schedule information. Several custom elements are depicted architecturally without enough fabrication or interface information to establish a reliable construction scope. These issues should be resolved through coordinated drawing and specification revisions rather than accumulated field decisions.
