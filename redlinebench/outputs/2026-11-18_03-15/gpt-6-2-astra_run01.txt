**Project context**  
Bonfire House — Chatham County, North Carolina; Chatham County Building Inspectors and Central Permitting; 2018 North Carolina Residential Code, based on the 2015 IRC with amendments; climate zone 4A.  
New two-story single-family residence, designated R-3, Type V-B; 3,270 sf heated plus a 570 sf garage, with a crawl space, decks, patios, well, septic system and above-ground lap pool.  
The architectural bid set dated 10/20/2021 includes general and code sheets, renderings, site and floor plans, roof plan, elevations, sections, assemblies, details, reflected ceiling plans and schedules; the survey was removed for review, and separate structural, civil, MEP and pool-engineering drawings are outside this review.  
The 11-page specification booklet is organized by trade and material rather than numbered divisions and covers sitework, foundations, framing, enclosure, finishes, equipment, building services, pool and utilities.

**Summary**  
The set communicates the design intent well, but it is not yet a dependable common basis for bids. The three greatest concerns are the **pool, deck and final-grade interfaces**; the **roof and wall water-control details**, particularly at low slopes and concealed steel; and **life-safety coordination** at the garage, sleeping rooms and pool enclosure. Before pricing is finalized, a coordinated addendum should fix the controlling elevations, establish the selected enclosure systems and protective assemblies, and reconcile the schedules with the enlarged plans and specifications.
