**Brief summary (3–5 sentences)**  
Overall, the set communicates the design intent but contains several coordination gaps that are likely to drive RFIs—especially around exterior railings/guards, datum/elevation control, and the garage/life-safety separations. The most significant disputes risk comes from spec-to-drawing conflicts (windows/doors, insulation targets, interior stone locations), which can directly impact procurement and pricing. The stair/guard information needs immediate attention due to apparent dimensional inconsistencies and missing code-critical criteria. Finally, several building-envelope details and site control items are under-specified or ambiguously sequenced, increasing the likelihood of water-management and field coordination problems.
