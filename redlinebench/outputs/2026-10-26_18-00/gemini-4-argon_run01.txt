### Step 1: Project Context

- **Project Name, Location, and Code Jurisdiction:** Bonfire House; Chatham County, North Carolina; 2018 North Carolina Residential Code (adopting the 2015 International Residential Code with NC amendments).
- **Building Type and Construction Type:** Two-story single-family residence over a sealed crawl space with an attached garage and outdoor lap pool; Occupancy Classification R-3, Construction Type V-B (unrated).
- **Climate Zone:** Climate Zone 4A (Mixed-Humid, per Sheet G0.11).
- **Scope of Drawing Set:** 34 sheets comprising Cover Sheet, General Sheets (G0.01, G0.11, G1.00), Architectural Site Plan (A1.00 placeholder, A1.01), Floor/Roof Plans (A2.00–A2.03), Exterior Elevations and Building/Wall Sections (A3.01–A3.22), Wall/Floor/Roof Assemblies and Exterior Details (A4.00–A4.13), Deck Plans/Elevations/Sections/Details (A5.01, A5.10, A5.20, A5.30), Reflected Ceiling Plan (A6.01), Interior Elevations and Details (A7.01, A7.02, A7.10), and Door/Window/Finish/Appliance Schedules (A8.01). Civil, structural, mechanical, electrical, and plumbing discipline drawings are not included.
- **Specification Divisions Included:** An 11-page narrative project specification booklet (*Bonfire House - Project Specifications*, dated 10/20/2021) organized by trade/building topic rather than CSI MasterFormat divisions.

### Summary

Overall, while the drawing set and specifications establish a clear architectural concept and include helpful enlarged plans and assembly details, the documents exhibit substantial coordination errors between sheets and between the drawings and project specifications that would generate numerous RFIs and change orders during construction. The **top three areas of greatest concern** are:
1. **Code and Life Safety Deficiencies:** Second Bedroom 09 has only a fixed picture window and no emergency escape/rescue opening; the garage-to-dwelling fire separation is compromised by a non-rated 4'-0" pocket door (`13-2`) directly into Office 13 and 1/2" gypsum board (instead of 5/8" Type X) beneath the second-floor living space; and exterior/interior stair guards are either undersized (`26"` cable railing on `4/A5.10`) or lack infill (`OPEN` guardrails on `A3.22` and `8/A7.01`).
2. **Schedule, Plan, and Elevation Conflicts:** Window tags, sizes, and counts on the exterior elevations (`A3.01`–`A3.03`) frequently contradict the floor plans (`A2.01A`, `A2.01B`, `A2.02`), the Window Schedule specifies Marvin Ultrex windows while the specifications require Andersen 400 Series, Door `11-1` and the Pantry door are omitted from the Door Schedule while nonexistent Door `10-1` is included, and Powder Room 14 is scheduled for carpet (`F-4`).
3. **Constructability, Envelope, and Dimensional Errors:** Pocket doors are drawn sliding into wall cavities occupied by recessed shelves (`2/A7.10`) and in-wall return air ducts, a fixed countertop is drawn over a specified top-loading washer (`13/A7.02`), multiple wall and floor assembly thicknesses (`E-2`, `E-3`, `E-4`, `F-2`, `F-3`) do not equal the sum of their constituent layers, Pool Section `1/A5.20` draws the pool deck 2'-0" too high (at Level 1 instead of `-2'-0"`), and low-slope/valley metal roof conditions (`6/A4.12`, `5/A4.13`) are improperly detailed for drainage and slope.
