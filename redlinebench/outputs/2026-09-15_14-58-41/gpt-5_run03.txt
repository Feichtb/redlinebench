Step 1 — Project context

- Name/Jurisdiction: Bonfire House, Chatham County, North Carolina; 2018 North Carolina Residential Code (adopts 2015 IRC) noted.
- Type/Construction: Single-family residence (R‑3), Type V‑B wood frame; attached garage.
- Climate zone: 4A (mixed-humid) stated.
- Drawing scope: Architectural only — general sheets, site, floor/roof plans, enlarged plans, exterior elevations, building/wall/ stair sections, assemblies, exterior/interior details, deck sheets, RCP/lighting, interior elevations, door/window schedules. No structural/MEP/civil/pool drawings included.
- Specifications: 11‑page narrative “Project Specifications – Description of Materials, Applications and Allowances”; not CSI-formatted; numerous product selections/allowances.

Summary

Overall, the set is well developed graphically but contains numerous coordination gaps between drawings and the narrative specs, unclear or inconsistent stair/roof geometry notes, and insufficient life-safety detailing at the garage separation and egress glazing. The most critical issues are: 1) specification-to-drawing conflicts (windows/doors, wall/insulation approach, radiant floor scope), 2) code and life safety omissions (garage separation, egress/safety glazing, stair compliance), and 3) building envelope clarity (unvented roof with vented soffits, incomplete transition details, and deck-to-wall waterproofing). These items will generate RFIs and change orders if not resolved before permit and pricing.
