## Project context

- **Project:** Bonfire House, Chatham County, North Carolina; drawings and specifications dated October 20, 2021.
- **Code/type:** 2018 North Carolina Residential Code, adopting the 2015 IRC with amendments; two-story single-family residence, R-3 occupancy, Type V-B construction.
- **Climate:** Zone 4A, as stated.
- **Documents:** General and architectural sheets covering site, plans, elevations, sections, assemblies, details, decks, lighting/RCP, interiors, and schedules; structural, civil, MEP, and specialist pool drawings are not included, and the survey is expressly removed.
- **Specifications:** Eleven-page narrative trade specification, not organized into numbered CSI divisions.

## Summary

The set communicates the architectural design but leaves significant coordination and installation decisions unresolved for a bid package. The three greatest concerns are **envelope continuity and wet-area waterproofing**, **garage separation and other life-safety documentation**, and **drawing/specification conflicts affecting products, dimensions, and pool/deck interfaces**. These issues should be reconciled in a coordinated revision rather than left to field interpretation or an informal document-precedence decision. Closing those gaps would materially reduce RFIs, inconsistent bids, and construction-stage changes.
