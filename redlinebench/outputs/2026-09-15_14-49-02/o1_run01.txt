SUMMARY  
Overall, the drawings and specs contain numerous coordination gaps—most notably between the finish schedule and specifications, and between the window/door schedules and the products required. Many code‐related clarifications are missing, especially for egress, fire separation, and stair geometry. The three greatest concerns are (1) the repeated spec‐versus‐drawings mismatches for finishes and major assemblies, (2) the questionable stair dimensions from crawl space to first floor, and (3) the lack of egress window in Bedroom #09.
