Step 1 – Project context  
• Project: “Bonfire House,” un-addressed parcel, Chatham County NC; jurisdiction 2018 NC Residential Code (IRC 2015 w/ amendments).  
• Occupancy R-3 single-family; Construction Type V-B.  
• Climate zone 4A (mixed-humid).  
• Drawing set: General (G), Architectural (A) only – plans, elevations, sections, wall/roof/floor assemblies, interior & exterior details, deck sheets, schedules.  No structural, MEP, civil, or landscape sheets.  
• Specifications: 11-page architect-written booklet (no CSI divisions) describing materials, allowances, and systems.

Summary  
The Bonfire House documents show an ambitious custom residence but contain numerous coordination and scope gaps. Key inconsistencies between drawings and the short-form specifications (windows, cladding, door materials) will cause immediate bidding confusion. Stair geometry, garage fire separation, and missing life-safety details present clear code compliance risks. Building-science detailing (WRB, flashing, insulation continuity) is incomplete, and many selections and systems are left “TBD,” leaving cost and constructability unresolved. Greatest concern areas are: (1) drawing-to-spec conflicts on major building envelope components, (2) incomplete or incorrect stair and code-required safety details, and (3) large undefined scopes for critical systems (mechanical, pool, renewable energy) that will drive change orders.
