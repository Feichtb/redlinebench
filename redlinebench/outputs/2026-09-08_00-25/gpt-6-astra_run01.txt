## Project context

- **Project:** Bonfire House, Chatham County, North Carolina; county permitting jurisdiction; stated code is the 2018 North Carolina Residential Code, adopting the 2015 IRC with amendments.
- **Building:** Two-story single-family residence with garage, crawlspace, decks and lap pool; classified R-3, Type V-B in the drawings.
- **Climate:** Zone 4A.
- **Documents:** Architectural bid set dated 10/20/2021, including general information, site and floor plans, elevations, sections, assemblies, details, reflected ceiling plans and schedules; survey removed for review; structural, civil, MEP and specialist pool drawings are not supplied.
- **Specifications:** Eleven-page, unnumbered trade-based booklet covering sitework, construction materials, finishes, equipment, building systems, pool and utilities rather than numbered specification divisions.

## Summary

The set communicates the architectural intent but contains substantial coordination and detailing gaps that would generate RFIs and inconsistent bids. The three greatest concerns are incomplete envelope and wet-area waterproofing details, unresolved garage/pool/egress life-safety documentation, and conflicts among schedules, drawings and specifications. Site datum control and deferred selections also expose the project to field adjustments and change orders. A coordinated revision should resolve these items before construction release and establish explicit pricing assumptions for any remaining deferred decisions.
