Step 1 — Project context

- Name/location/jurisdiction: Bonfire House, Chatham County, North Carolina; drawings cite 2018 North Carolina Residential Code (adopts 2015 IRC).
- Building/Construction type: Single-family dwelling (R‑3), Type V‑B (per code summary).
- Climate zone: 4A (stated).
- Drawing set scope: Architectural only — general notes/3D, site plan, crawl/Level 1/Level 2/roof plans (with enlarged areas), exterior elevations, building and wall sections, exterior/deck/interior details, reflected ceiling plans, interior elevations, door/window and room-finish schedules. No structural, MEP, civil, or landscape drawing packages included (though frequently referenced).
- Specifications: A separate 11‑page “Project Specifications – Description of Materials, Applications and Allowances” was provided (not a full CSI-format spec book).

Summary

This is a thoughtful architectural concept set with many helpful details, but it contains significant coordination gaps and conflicts that will generate RFIs during bidding and construction. The most critical issues are: (1) stair geometry errors that don’t match the 10‑ft floor‑to‑floor height, (2) contradictory specifications and drawings for windows, siding systems, and insulation assemblies, and (3) incomplete life‑safety detailing at the garage separation and inconsistent guard/handrail information. Clarifying the building envelope (WRB, flashing, and sill pans), aligning specs with the drawings (manufacturers, materials, R‑values), and resolving finish schedule inconsistencies (Entry Hall and Reading Nook) should be prioritized.
