## Summary

The package communicates the architectural concept well but is not sufficiently coordinated for reliable fixed-price bidding and construction without substantial clarification. The three greatest concerns are **building-envelope and wet-area detailing**, **garage separation and residential life-safety documentation**, and **drawing-to-specification conflicts affecting major product and scope selections**. Dimensional discrepancies, unresolved custom-window interfaces, and incomplete pool/deck coordination would also generate field RFIs and change-order exposure. These issues should be resolved in a coordinated architectural revision before procurement and construction.
