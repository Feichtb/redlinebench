## Step 1: Project context

- **Project:** Bonfire House, Chatham County, NC (unaddressed parcel, Residential District 1). Code is the 2018 North Carolina Residential Code (IRC 2015 with amendments).
- **Building type:** New two-story single-family house (R-3, Type V-B, wood frame) on a CMU sealed crawl space, with attached garage, decks and an above-ground lap pool.
- **Climate zone:** 4A (stated on G0.11).
- **Drawing scope:** Architectural only — G0.01–G1.00, A1.00–A8.01 (site, plans, elevations, sections, assemblies, details, deck, RCP, interior elevations, schedules). A1.00 Survey is intentionally blank. Structural, MEP and civil sheets are not included.
- **Specifications:** An 11-page narrative "Description of Materials" (not CSI-formatted) covering site, foundation, framing, exterior, roofing, insulation, finishes, HVAC, plumbing, electrical/low voltage, appliances, pool, septic, well and utilities.

I could not reliably verify element counts from elevation graphics at this resolution. Counts below come from tags and schedules in the sheet text.

## Summary

The set is graphically well developed, and most primary dimension strings close. However, the specification and the Revit-generated schedules were evidently not reconciled with each other or with the details, and the general notes contain uncorrected boilerplate.

The three areas of greatest concern are:

1. **Spec/drawing/schedule conflicts on priced items.** These include the window manufacturer, wall sheathing and insulation product, cladding locations, door 03-1, the Powder Room and Entry Hall finishes, hose bib count, solar scope and pool construction.
2. **Roof and wall envelope design.** Concerns are diagonal-slope standing seam on furring with no solid deck, very low-slope porch and entry roofs, the roof-to-wall intersection between wings, sheathing outboard of foam, paving flush against wood framing, and no below-grade dampproofing.
3. **Life-safety documentation.** Gaps are the garage ceiling separation under the guest suite, emergency escape openings for Bedrooms 09 and 17, safety glazing, stair and deck guard details, and the pool barrier.

Resolving these before bid would remove a large share of likely RFIs and change orders.
