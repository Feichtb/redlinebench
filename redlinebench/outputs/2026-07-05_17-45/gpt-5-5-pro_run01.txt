Summary: The documents communicate a distinctive design intent, but the set has significant coordination gaps between plans, schedules, details, and the narrative specifications. The three greatest concern areas are envelope/building-science coordination, especially low-slope standing-seam roofs and sealed crawl-space conflicts; code/life-safety documentation, especially garage separation, glazing safety, egress, stairs, and pool barriers; and specification/drawing conflicts for windows, insulation, finishes, doors, lighting, and roof slopes. The custom architectural elements—entry glazing/woodwork, pool/deck, bunk nook, rain chains, and low-voltage/solar infrastructure—need additional details before construction pricing and field execution.
