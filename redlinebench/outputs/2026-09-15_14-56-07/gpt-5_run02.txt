Step 1 – Project context

- Project: Bonfire House, Chatham County, North Carolina; jurisdiction per 2018 North Carolina Residential Code (adopts IRC 2015).
- Building: New single-family residence (R‑3), Type V‑B construction; two stories over sealed crawl space with attached garage.
- Climate zone: IECC Zone 4A (mixed‑humid, from NC location).
- Drawing set scope: Architectural only (general info, site plan, floor plans including enlarged areas, roof plan, exterior elevations, building/ wall/ stair sections, exterior and deck details, interior elevations/details, RCPs, door/window and finish schedules). No structural/MEP/civil sheets included.
- Specifications: An 11‑page project specification/outline spec provided; no formal CSI divisions or technical sections.

Summary

Overall, the documents contain numerous coordination gaps between drawings and the outline specifications, significant stair geometry errors, and incomplete life‑safety/separation information at the garage interface. The building envelope details lack clear, continuous air/water management strategies, especially at windows, bases, and complex overhangs. The top concerns are: (1) code/life‑safety compliance for stairs and garage separation (riser geometry, ceiling gypsum, and rated self‑closing door), (2) major spec/drawing conflicts (window manufacturer/materials, wall/roof insulation strategies, cladding types), and (3) incomplete/ambiguous detailing that will drive RFIs (window/door water management, deck–pool interfaces, controls scope, and numerous TBD items).
