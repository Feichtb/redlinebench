### Step 1 — Project context

- **Project:** Bonfire House; unaddressed property in Chatham County, North Carolina; 2018 North Carolina Residential Code, based on the 2015 IRC with amendments.
- **Building:** Two-story, four-bedroom single-family residence, R-3 occupancy, identified as Type V-B construction.
- **Climate:** Climate Zone 4A.
- **Drawing scope:** General/code sheets and architectural site, floor, roof, elevation, section, assembly, detail, reflected-ceiling, interior-elevation, and schedule sheets; structural, civil, landscape, mechanical, plumbing, electrical, and pool-engineering drawings are not included.
- **Specifications:** Narrative specifications cover sitework, foundations, framing, envelope, openings, finishes, cabinetry, HVAC, plumbing, electrical/low-voltage, appliances, landscaping, pool, septic, well, propane, and utilities, but are not organized into formal specification divisions.

### Summary

The documents communicate the architectural concept well but contain significant coordination gaps that are likely to generate bidding qualifications and construction RFIs. The three greatest concerns are the conflicting envelope and insulation assemblies, incomplete code-related information for garage separation, egress, guards, and pool barriers, and unresolved scope or product conflicts between the drawings and specifications. Site grading, pool/deck interfaces, custom window construction, and low-slope roofing also require substantial clarification before construction. The schedules and cross-references need a coordinated revision so that room numbers, finishes, products, and sheet references provide a single reliable basis for pricing and construction.
