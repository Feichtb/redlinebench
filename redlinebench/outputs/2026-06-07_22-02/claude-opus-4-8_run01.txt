# Document Review: Bonfire House — BID SET (10/20/2021)

## Step 1: Project Context

- **Project / Location / Code:** Bonfire House, unaddressed parcel in Chatham County, NC; 2018 North Carolina Residential Code (adopts IRC 2015 with amendments).
- **Building / Construction type:** New single-family dwelling, R-3 occupancy, Type V-B.
- **Climate zone:** 4A (mixed-humid), per drawings.
- **Drawing scope:** Architectural only (G-series general, A-series: site, plans, elevations, building/wall sections, assemblies, exterior/interior details, deck, RCP, schedules). Structural is referenced but **not included**; no MEP, civil, or landscape sheets. Survey sheet (A1.00) intentionally blank.
- **Specifications:** A narrative "Description of Materials, Applications and Allowances" (11 pages), not CSI-format divisions. Provided and reviewed against the drawings.

## Summary

The set is reasonably complete for a custom residence and internally well-organized, but it carries a consistent pattern of **drawing-to-specification conflicts** and **schedule errors** that will generate RFIs and change orders. The three areas of greatest concern are: (1) **Roofing** — a snap-lock standing-seam product specified over 2:12, 1/4:12, and ~1/8:12 slopes that fall below its minimum slope, a genuine performance/leak risk; (2) **Garage life-safety separation** — habitable space over the garage with only 1/2" gypsum shown and no rated/self-closing door identified; and (3) **Material/product conflicts** — windows (Marvin vs. Andersen), door 03-1 (metal vs. wood), continuous-insulation type and thickness, and three rooms where the finish schedule (tile/carpet) contradicts the spec (stone). Secondary but important: fixture-count and insulation-value discrepancies between G0.11 and the spec, an undefined 4th bedroom, sub-minimum railing heights, and pervasive deferral of pool/deck/structural coordination. A coordinated pass reconciling the schedules (A8.01) and code summary (G0.11) against the narrative specification would resolve the majority of these findings before bid.
