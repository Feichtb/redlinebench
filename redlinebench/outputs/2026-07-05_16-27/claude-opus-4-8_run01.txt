## Step 1: Project Context

- **Project:** Bonfire House — new single-family residence, un-addressed parcel, Chatham County, North Carolina. Code: **2018 North Carolina Residential Code (adopts IRC 2015)**; jurisdiction Chatham County Central Permitting.
- **Building/Construction type:** Single-family dwelling, IRC use (drawings also mislabel it R-3 / Type V-B per IBC). Two stories over a sealed crawl space, 3,840 SF total (3,270 SF heated + 570 SF garage).
- **Climate zone:** 4A (stated on G0.11).
- **Scope of set:** General (G0.01, G0.11, G1.00), Architectural (survey, site, plans, elevations, sections, wall/horizontal assemblies, exterior/interior/deck details, RCP, interior elevations, door/window/finish/appliance schedules). **Absent disciplines:** Structural (referenced as "see structural set"), MEP, and civil are not in this set — not reviewed per scope.
- **Specifications:** An 11-page narrative "Description of Materials, Applications and Allowances" is provided and was used for cross-checking.

## Summary

The set is reasonably complete and well-organized graphically, but it carries the coordination gaps typical of a bid set assembled from templates and an evolving spec: schedules and the narrative specification disagree in several substantive places, and the code-summary sheet still contains commercial (IBC) boilerplate inappropriate to an IRC dwelling. The most consequential issues are **life-safety/envelope items at the garage** (no identified fire-rated garage/dwelling wall + door separation and no rated ceiling under the habitable guest suite), **spec-to-drawing material conflicts** (Marvin vs. Andersen windows, west-facade cladding listed twice, 2x8 vs. 2x6 exterior walls, 1/2" vs. 2" crawl-wall insulation, ceiling R-value math), and **railing/guard/pool-barrier deficiencies** (26"/36" rails vs. required 36"/48"). Secondary but real problems include the missing door-schedule row (11-1), the carpet-in-a-powder-room finish error, the mislabeled guest-bath elevation, the 501 vs. 502 crawl datum, and a spec that both includes solar and calls the building only "solar ready." The top three areas of concern are: **(1) garage separation and fire-rated assemblies**, **(2) reconciling the specification against the drawings on envelope materials and insulation values**, and **(3) guard/handrail/pool-barrier heights and stair/egress detailing.** These should be resolved on the documents before bidding to limit RFIs and change orders.
