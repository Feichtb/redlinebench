# Construction Document Review — Bonfire House

## Step 1: Project Context

- **Project:** Bonfire House, Bid Set dated 10/20/2021; un-addressed parcel in Chatham County, North Carolina. Jurisdiction: Chatham County Building Inspectors/Central Permitting; 2018 NC Residential Code (IRC 2015 with amendments).
- **Building type:** New single-family residence, R-3 occupancy, Type V-B construction; two stories, 3,840 SF total (3,270 SF heated), 4 bedrooms, 3.5 baths, crawl space foundation, attached garage, above-ground lap pool and decks.
- **Climate zone:** 4A (stated on G0.11, consistent with central NC).
- **Drawing set scope:** General (G) and Architectural (A) sheets only — plans, elevations, sections, assemblies, details, RCP, schedules. Structural, MEP, and civil drawings are referenced but not included; site survey removed for this review.
- **Specifications:** A single narrative "Description of Materials" document (not CSI division format), covering sitework through appliances.

## Summary

This is a well-organized small set with strong graphic quality, but it carries a high density of coordination errors between the specification and the drawings — most critically the wholesale product conflicts (Andersen vs. Marvin windows, ZIP-R vs. plywood-plus-rigid walls, ½" vs. 2" crawl-space insulation) and unreconciled facade cladding assignments, any of which would generate immediate bid questions or wrong procurement. The three areas of greatest concern are: (1) the roof system, where the drawn assembly lacks underlayment and a walkable substrate, and snap-lock panels are shown at slopes down to 1/8":12 with conflicting slope callouts across sheets; (2) life-safety gaps, particularly the missing Type X garage separation below the guest suite, unrated garage doors, the 26" deck guard, and undemonstrated bedroom egress; and (3) unresolved scope — pervasive TBDs, bidder-design radiant flooring incompatible with the drawn floor assembly, and admittedly approximate grading on which decks, stairs, and the pool depend. A thorough spec/drawing reconciliation pass and an envelope/roof redesign review are recommended before this set is priced.
