## Project context

- **Project:** Bonfire House, Chatham County, North Carolina; governing code stated as the 2018 North Carolina Residential Code, adopting the 2015 IRC with amendments.
- **Building:** New two-story single-family residence with garage, crawlspace, decks and lap pool; classified in the drawings as R-3, Type V-B.
- **Climate:** Zone 4A, as stated.
- **Documents:** General information, architectural site/floor/roof plans, elevations, sections, assemblies, details, reflected ceiling plans and schedules; structural, civil and dedicated MEP drawings are not included and are not reviewed here.
- **Specifications:** Eleven-page narrative material/application specification dated October 20, 2021; not organized into numbered CSI divisions.

## Summary

The set communicates the architectural design but contains substantial coordination and detailing gaps that would generate RFIs and inconsistent bids. The greatest concerns are **envelope waterproofing and enclosure continuity**, particularly showers, low-slope roofs and flush thresholds; **life-safety documentation**, especially garage separation, bedroom escape windows and pool barriers; and **conflicting assembly, product and dimensional information** across drawings and specifications. Unresolved custom fabrication details and finish selections add further pricing and construction risk. These matters should be reconciled in a coordinated architectural revision before construction release.
