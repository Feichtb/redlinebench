## 1. Project context

- **Project:** Bonfire House, unaddressed property in Chatham County, North Carolina; Chatham County permitting jurisdiction; drawings cite the 2018 North Carolina Residential Code, based on the 2015 IRC with amendments.
- **Building:** Two-story single-family residence with attached garage, crawl space, decks and lap pool; designated R-3 occupancy and Type V-B construction.
- **Climate:** Zone 4A, stated on G0.11.
- **Documents:** 34-sheet architectural bid set and 11-page narrative specification booklet, both dated October 20, 2021; drawings include plans, elevations, sections, assemblies, details and schedules; survey is expressly removed, and separate structural, civil and MEP drawings are not supplied.
- **Specifications:** Unnumbered trade sections covering sitework, foundations, framing, enclosure, finishes, cabinetry, equipment, plumbing, HVAC, electrical, pool and utilities—not a numbered CSI project manual.

## 3. Summary

The set communicates the design intent but contains substantial unresolved coordination and detailing for construction. The three greatest concerns are **garage separation and other life-safety details**, **waterproofing and enclosure continuity**, and **conflicting product, finish and dimensional requirements between drawings and specifications**. The pool, exterior stairs and custom interior work also require more explicit interface and fabrication information. A coordinated revision should resolve these items before procurement and construction rather than leaving contractors to establish precedence through RFIs.
