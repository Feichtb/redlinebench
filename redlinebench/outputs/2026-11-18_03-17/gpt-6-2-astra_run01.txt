### Project context

**Bonfire House** — new two-story, single-family residence in Chatham County, North Carolina; the parcel was not yet addressed. G0.11 identifies Chatham County Building Inspectors and Central Permitting, the 2018 North Carolina Residential Code (2015 IRC with amendments), occupancy R-3, construction Type V-B, and Climate Zone 4A.

**Documents reviewed:** The October 20, 2021 architectural bid set includes general information, renderings, site and floor plans, roof plan, elevations, sections, assemblies, details, deck and pool drawings, reflected ceiling plans, and schedules. The survey was expressly withheld from this review; no separate structural, civil, MEP, or pool-engineering sets were supplied. The accompanying 11-page specification is organized by trade rather than numbered divisions and covers sitework, structure, enclosure, finishes, equipment, utilities, and pool coordination.

### Summary

The set communicates the design intent well, but several decisions that affect bids, rough openings, and finished elevations are split across documents that give different answers. The first priority is life safety: resolve the pool barrier and direct bedroom access, the garage separation below the guest suite, and the escape opening for each intended bedroom. The second is water management, especially the low-slope porch draining toward the house, the shower waterproofing, and the nearly flush exterior thresholds. The third is procurement and scope: issue a coordinated addendum fixing the window product and count, cladding locations, insulation buildups, and whether the contractor is pricing an installed solar-and-battery system or only provisions for one.
