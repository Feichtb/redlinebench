## Step 1: Project Context

- **Project / Location / Code:** Bonfire House, an unaddressed 9.39-acre parcel in Chatham County, NC. The code is the 2018 North Carolina Residential Code (based on the 2015 IRC), with energy compliance per NCRC Table N1102.1.2. The Zoning block also cites IBC Table 503.
- **Building Type:** New two-story single-family detached house with sealed crawl space and attached garage, plus deck and lap pool. R-3 occupancy, Type V-B construction. 3,840 SF total, 3,270 SF heated.
- **Climate Zone:** 4A (stated).
- **Drawing Scope:**
  - G-series: symbols, code summary, renderings.
  - A-series: survey placeholder (content removed), site plan, crawl/floor/roof plans, enlarged plans, exterior elevations, building and wall sections, wall and horizontal assemblies, exterior details, deck plan/elevations/sections/details, RCP, interior elevations and details, door/window/finish/appliance schedules.
  - Absent: structural, civil, MEP/FP, landscape, and survey content.
- **Specifications:** An 11-page narrative "Description of Materials, Applications and Allowances." It is not in CSI format and has no numbered divisions.

## Summary

The set is well organized and graphically clear, but coordination between drawings, schedules, and the narrative specification is weak. Conflicts in window manufacturer, cladding locations, exterior door types, insulation values, framing sizes, and scope (solar, pool) will drive early RFIs and pricing disputes. The top three areas of concern are:

1. **Wet-area and envelope assemblies:** no WRB in E-3, no shower waterproofing, tile on bare plywood with radiant heat unaccommodated, sub-minimum-slope snap-lock roofs, and the steel porch penetration.
2. **Life-safety documentation:** garage ceiling separation, unidentified egress openings and fourth bedroom, missing safety glazing, and inadequate guard, stair, and pool-barrier information.
3. **Schedule accuracy:** finish, door, and fixture-count errors, plus numerous TBDs, owner questions, and unfunded allowances in the specification.
