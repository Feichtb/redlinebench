## Summary

This bid set is graphically well developed but suffers from significant coordination gaps between the drawings, the schedules, and the narrative specification—most seriously in product basis (windows, sheathing/insulation system, exterior cladding assignment, door materials) where the two documents describe different buildings. The three areas of greatest concern are: (1) the roof system—a snap-lock standing seam roof on furring over foam without a nail base, applied to low-slope areas below the manufacturer's minimum, with conflicting vented/unvented soffit intent; (2) schedule integrity—the door schedule misses a plan door, the finish schedule assigns carpet to a powder room and wrong wall finishes to the wood-clad rooms, and no glazing, rating, or ceiling information is provided; and (3) life-safety/code items left undefined—garage ceiling separation under the guest suite, emergency escape and safety glazing identification, and a 26" railing at exterior stairs. Resolving the wall assembly dimension/callout conflicts and the entry-hall header/roof-slope geometry before bid would eliminate a large fraction of likely RFIs.
