Step 1 – Project context

- Project: Bonfire House, Chatham County, North Carolina; jurisdiction: 2018 North Carolina Residential Code (adopts IRC 2015) per G0.11.
- Building type and construction: Single-family residence (R-3), Type V-B, 2 stories with attached garage and exterior decks/pool.
- Climate zone: 4A (Mixed-humid) – stated on G0.11.
- Drawing set scope: Architectural only – general notes, site plan, floor plans, enlarged plans, roof plan, exterior elevations, building sections, wall sections, exterior/interior details, deck plans/sections/details, reflected ceiling plans, interior elevations, door/window schedules and room finish schedule. No structural, MEP, or civil drawing packages are included (only referenced).
- Specifications: A short project “Specifications – Description of Materials, Applications and Allowances” (11 pages) – narrative format; not organized by CSI divisions.

Summary

Overall, the documents are schematic-to-design‑development level in many areas rather than fully coordinated construction documents. The most serious issues are: (1) drawing-specification mismatches for exterior wall/roof assemblies and windows (manufacturer and R-values), (2) code/life-safety gaps and inconsistencies at stairs, garage separation/door, and guards/pool barrier, and (3) incomplete water-management details for windows, door thresholds, and roof assemblies. These gaps will generate RFIs and pricing uncertainty and pose risks for envelope performance and inspections. The set will benefit from a coordinated update of assemblies, final product selections, complete stair geometry, and full waterproofing details at openings and transitions.
