SUMMARY  
The architectural bid set is visually complete but reveals numerous coordination and information gaps. Greatest concerns are: (1) inconsistent or missing life-safety provisions (garage separation, egress windows, stair geometry); (2) major conflicts between the narrative specifications and the drawing schedules (windows, doors, insulation, cladding), which will drive pricing disputes; (3) critical missing drawings/details referenced but not issued (sheet A3.11, structural connections, mechanical/electrical device layouts). Resolving these issues before issuance for construction will greatly reduce RFIs and change orders.
