### Project context

**Bonfire House** — New two-story, single-family residence in Chatham County, North Carolina; 3,270 ft² heated plus a 570 ft² garage. G0.11 identifies R-3 occupancy, Type V-B construction, Climate Zone 4A, and the 2018 North Carolina Residential Code, based on the 2015 IRC with amendments.  
**Documents reviewed** — The October 20, 2021 architectural bid set includes general information, site and floor plans, roof and reflected ceiling plans, elevations, sections, assemblies, details, and schedules; the survey was expressly removed for this review, and separate structural, civil, MEP, and pool-engineering sets were not supplied. The accompanying 11-page specification is organized by trade rather than numbered divisions and covers sitework, foundation, framing, envelope, finishes, equipment, services, pool, utilities, and coordination.

### Summary

The set communicates the architectural intent well, but several discrepancies prevent reliable bidding or fabrication directly from the documents. The three greatest concerns are **the garage separation and unresolved sleeping-room designations**, **the low-slope metal roofs and incomplete water-management details**, and **the pool/deck barrier, grading, and vendor interfaces**. Before bids are compared, an addendum should also reconcile the window order, door and finish schedules, controlling dimensions, and allowance amounts so that bidders are pricing the same building.
