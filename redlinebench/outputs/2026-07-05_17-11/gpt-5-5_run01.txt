### Summary

The documents describe a clear design intent but contain many coordination gaps typical of a partially developed architectural/bid set. The most significant concerns are envelope detailing at low-slope metal roofs and custom wall/window conditions, garage/pool/stair life-safety documentation, and inconsistencies between specifications and drawings for major products and finishes. The top three risk areas for RFIs/change orders are: **low-slope roof/envelope detailing**, **pool/deck/guardrail/barrier coordination**, and **specification conflicts for windows, insulation, doors, finishes, and electrical/low-voltage systems**.
