## Step 1: Project Context

- **Project:** Bonfire House — new single-family residence, un-addressed parcel, Chatham County, NC. Code: 2018 North Carolina Residential Code (IRC 2015 w/ amendments); G0.11 also cites IBC Table 503.
- **Building/Construction type:** R-3 single-family, 2 stories, Type V-B, ~3,840 SF total (3,270 heated), attached garage, lap pool and decks.
- **Climate zone:** 4A (stated on G0.11).
- **Drawing scope:** General (G0.01, G0.11, G1.00) and Architectural only (site, crawl/floor/roof plans, exterior/interior elevations, building/wall sections, assemblies, details, deck sheets, RCP, schedules). Survey removed. No structural, MEP, civil, or landscape drawings (structural referenced but not included).
- **Specifications:** Narrative "Description of Materials, Applications and Allowances" (11 pp.), not CSI-formatted; no numbered divisions.

## Summary

The set is graphically well developed for a residence of this size, but coordination between the drawings and the narrative specification is weak: windows, exterior doors, sheathing system, insulation values, cladding locations, stud sizes and roof slopes are described differently in the two documents, and the finish schedule contradicts both the interior elevations and the spec in several rooms. Envelope assemblies contain internal errors (E-3 without a weather barrier, conflicting rigid insulation thicknesses, vented soffits on an unvented compact roof) that will generate RFIs. The three areas of greatest concern are: (1) drawing/spec product and assembly conflicts (windows, ZIP vs. plywood+foam, roofing slope minimums); (2) life-safety gaps at the garage separation, garage step-down, bedroom egress and glazing; and (3) unresolved grade, pool and mechanical scope combined with pervasive "TBD" items that prevent a firm bid.
