# CONSTRUCTION DOCUMENT REVIEW — BONFIRE HOUSE

## Step 1: Project Context

- **Project:** Bonfire House, un-addressed parcel, Chatham County, North Carolina. Jurisdiction: Chatham County Building Inspections / Central Permitting. Code: 2018 North Carolina Residential Code (adopts IRC 2015 w/ amendments). Bid Set dated 10/20/2021.
- **Building type / construction type:** New single-family dwelling, R-3 occupancy, Type V-B; 2 stories over a 6'-0" sealed crawl space; 3,270 SF heated + 570 SF garage; 4 bedrooms.
- **Climate zone:** 4A (stated on G0.11; consistent with Chatham County, NC).
- **Scope of set:** Architectural only — cover, symbols/abbreviations, code summary, 3D views, (blank) survey, site plan, floor plans (crawl, L1, enlarged L1 N/S, L2, roof), exterior elevations, building/wall sections, wall & horizontal assemblies, exterior details, deck plan/elevations/sections/details, RCP, interior elevations/details, door-window-finish-appliance schedules. **Absent disciplines:** structural, civil, MEP, landscape, pool (all referenced as by others / bidder design). Sheet A1.00 (Survey) is blank — site/topographic information cannot be verified in this review.
- **Specifications:** A narrative 11-page "Description of Materials, Applications and Allowances," not CSI MasterFormat divisions. No allowance dollar values are given.

**Stated limitations of this review:** Several sheets contain small text and overlapping dimension strings; where I could not confirm a count or dimension with confidence I have said so and assigned Moderate confidence rather than asserting an error. Window and door tag counts per elevation could not be fully verified at the provided resolution.

## Summary

The set is graphically coherent and unusually well developed for a single-family project in its exterior detailing, wall and floor assembly documentation, and interior elevations. However, it carries a substantial coordination deficit between the narrative specification and the drawings — the two documents disagree on window manufacturer, exterior cladding facade assignments, insulation systems, PV scope, door materials, hose bib counts, and insulation R-values, and the specification itself contains unanswered questions and "under consideration" scope that make it unsuitable for competitive bidding as written.

The three areas of greatest concern are: **(1) life-safety documentation gaps** — no safety glazing designations, no smoke/CO alarm locations, no identified emergency escape openings, no stair or guard details, and a garage-to-dwelling separation (below habitable rooms) shown with 1/2" gypsum board and an unidentified, unrated door; **(2) stair and vertical circulation geometry** — the two stair sections cannot be reconciled with the 10'-0" floor-to-floor, no stair plan exists, and both interior and exterior stairs lack riser, tread, headroom, and handrail information; and **(3) envelope control-layer continuity** — the roof weather barrier is shown below the deck with no underlayment above it, vented soffits are detailed against an unvented compact roof, the exterior rigid insulation and WRB have no detailed transition to the interior-face foundation insulation at the rim, and snap-lock standing seam roofing is applied to 1/8:12 and 1/4:12 planes. Resolving the schedule/finish conflicts on A8.01, completing the door and window schedules with hardware and glazing data, and issuing a stair package should be treated as prerequisites to release.
