# Step 1: Project Context

- **Project:** Bonfire House, Chatham County, North Carolina (Residential District 1). Code: 2018 NC Residential Code (IRC 2015 w/ amendments); jurisdiction Chatham County Building Inspections/Central Permitting.
- **Building type:** New single-family residence, R-3 occupancy, Type V-B construction, 2 stories, 3,840 SF total / 3,270 SF heated, with above-ground lap pool, deck, well, and septic.
- **Climate zone:** 4A (stated on G0.11).
- **Drawing set scope:** General (G0.01, G0.11, G1.00) and Architectural (A1–A8 series) only. Structural, MEP, civil, and pool drawings are referenced but not included; survey removed from A1.00.
- **Specifications:** Narrative "Description of Materials" format (11 pages), not organized by CSI divisions.

## Summary

This is a well-organized and graphically thorough architectural set, but it is not yet coordinated tightly enough for bidding: the specifications and drawings disagree on fundamental products (window manufacturer, sheathing/insulation system, crawl space insulation, exterior cladding assignments, door materials), and the schedules contain outright errors (missing door 11-1, carpet in the powder room, the mislabeled guest bathroom, the A5.11/A5.20 sheet-number mismatch). The three areas of greatest concern are: (1) life-safety gaps — undesignated bedroom egress openings, 1/2" gypsum at the garage ceiling below a bedroom, missing safety glazing designations, and the 26" railing callout; (2) the roof system — low-slope areas below the snap-lock panel's minimum slope combined with an assembly lacking a continuous deck/underlayment beneath the metal; and (3) the volume of unresolved design decisions (HVAC system, pool retaining wall, lighting fixtures, hardware, colors) that will generate change orders if bid as-is. A full spec-to-drawing reconciliation pass and a code/egress markup are recommended before this set is released for construction.
