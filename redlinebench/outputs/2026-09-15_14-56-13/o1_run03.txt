STEP 3: SUMMARY

Overall, the documents contain multiple brand and assembly mismatches (especially for windows and exterior siding), plus discrepancies in stair geometry and incomplete references to structural conditions. The most critical issues concern contradictory cladding instructions for west elevations, window brand conflicts, and missing data for garage‐to‐house separation. Greater alignment between the specification and the drawings is urgently needed to avoid RFIs and project delays.
