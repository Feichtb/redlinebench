### Brief summary (overall quality + top concerns)

The set communicates the overall design intent well (plans/elevations/assemblies are present), but it contains numerous coordination gaps that are likely to generate RFIs and scope disputes during bidding and construction. The most serious issues are (1) **internal inconsistencies in life-safety-critical items** (guard heights and stair arithmetic), (2) **major specification-to-drawing conflicts** (windows/doors, wall system and insulation approach), and (3) **missing constructible information** at high-risk interfaces (roof intersections, rain-chain drainage routing, removable pool-cover deck, and key penetration/sealing details). Before pricing or permit, the documents need a focused reconciliation pass on schedules, envelope assemblies, and the deck/pool safety details.
