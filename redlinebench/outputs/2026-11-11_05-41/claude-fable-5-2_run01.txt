## Step 1: Project context

- **Project / jurisdiction:** Bonfire House, new single-family house on an unaddressed 9.39-acre parcel in Chatham County, NC, under the 2018 NC Residential Code (IRC 2015 with amendments); Bid Set dated 10/20/2021.
- **Building / construction type:** R-3, Type V-B, two-storey wood frame on a CMU sealed crawlspace, with a slab-on-grade garage, decks and an above-ground lap pool.
- **Climate zone:** 4A (stated on G0.11).
- **Drawing scope:** General (G0.01–G1.00) and Architectural (A1.00–A8.01) only. The survey sheet is intentionally blank. No structural, MEP, civil or landscape sheets are included, so those disciplines are not reviewed.
- **Specifications:** An 11-page narrative "Description of Materials, Applications and Allowances". It is not organized by CSI division.

Reading limits: window-tag counts and some small plan tags could not be fully verified at this resolution. Findings that depend on them are rated Moderate or say so.

## Summary

The set communicates design intent well, and most dimension strings close. It is not yet coordinated to bid-set standard: the schedules (door, finish, window, lighting) contain numerous errors against the plans and elevations, and the narrative spec conflicts with the drawings on basic products and scope.

The three areas of greatest concern are:

1. **Roof and envelope.** The diagonal low-slope standing seam roof has no solid deck under the panels, treated furring against Galvalume, and under-filled joist cavities. The 1/4:12 porch roof, flush thresholds against wood framing, missing shower and below-grade waterproofing, and the conflict between ZIP-R and plywood-over-foam walls add to this.
2. **Life safety.** Second Bedroom 09 appears to have no escape opening, the garage ceiling under the guest bedroom is 1/2" gypsum, no safety glazing is designated, the pool barrier is undefined, and stair and guard information is thin.
3. **Scope and specification conflicts.** The window manufacturer, PV and battery scope, pool shell responsibility, subfloor and sheathing products, and the unverified site datum will all generate RFIs and pricing disputes.
