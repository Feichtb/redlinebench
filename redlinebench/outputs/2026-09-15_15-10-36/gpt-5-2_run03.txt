### Brief summary (3–5 sentences)

Overall, the set communicates the architectural intent, but it contains several high-risk coordination problems that are very likely to generate RFIs and change orders. The most critical issues are (1) major **spec-to-drawing procurement conflicts** (windows/doors, cabling category, insulation approach), (2) **inconsistent vertical datums and guard/railing heights** across sheets, and (3) **finish schedule errors/ambiguities** (notably the Powder Room floor and wood-finish room treatments). Building-envelope execution details for a sealed crawlspace and continuous exterior insulation are not sufficiently resolved to avoid field interpretation. Tightening the schedules, unifying datums, and reconciling the spec selections with the drawing tags should be prioritized before bidding/construction.
