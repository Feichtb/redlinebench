STEP 3: SUMMARY  
Overall, these documents reveal multiple inconsistencies between the drawings and specifications, especially for door/window products, stair geometry, and required garage separation detailing. The mismatch in brand names and material descriptions (Marvin vs Andersen, Jeld Wen vs metal door) is a key coordination error. Stair dimensions and the unaddressed fire‐rating at the garage boundary also pose significant code and cost risks. The three most critical concerns are the contradictory window/door information, the incorrect stair height calculations, and the missing garage‐to‐house fire separation requirements.
