## Step 1 — Project context

- **Project:** Bonfire House; unaddressed property in Chatham County, North Carolina; 2018 North Carolina Residential Code, based on the 2015 IRC with amendments.
- **Building:** Two-story, four-bedroom single-family residence, R-3 occupancy, Type V-B construction, with garage, crawl space, decks, patios, pool, well, and septic system.
- **Climate:** Zone 4A.
- **Drawing scope:** General and architectural sheets including site plan, plans, elevations, sections, assemblies, details, reflected ceiling plans, interior elevations, and schedules; structural, civil, landscape, pool, and dedicated MEP drawings were not provided.
- **Specifications:** An informal 11-page project specification covering sitework, foundations, framing, envelope, finishes, cabinetry, HVAC, plumbing, electrical/low-voltage, appliances, pool, septic, well, propane, utilities, and coordination; it is not organized into conventional specification divisions.

## Summary

The documents communicate the overall design intent well but are not fully coordinated for construction, particularly at envelope assemblies, garage separation, stairs, pool barriers, and site layout. The most significant concerns are the conflicting insulation and cladding requirements, incomplete code and construction information at the garage/stairs/pool, and unresolved site, pool, and custom-detail geometry. Numerous TBD selections and optional systems also prevent a stable basis of bid. A coordinated revision incorporating final product selections, corrected schedules and datums, completed life-safety details, and resolved envelope transitions would substantially reduce RFIs and change-order exposure.
