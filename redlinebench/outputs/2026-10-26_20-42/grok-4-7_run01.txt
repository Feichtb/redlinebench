**Summary**

This bid set is not ready to price or build without a substantial coordination revision. The narrative specification and the drawings describe different products and assemblies for windows, exterior cladding, wall and roof insulation, several floor finishes, hose bibs, and the garage door, and the energy values on the code summary do not match either the specification math or the drawn assemblies. The interior stair, as drawn, cannot be built to a legal riser height, the two stair sections contradict each other, and headroom, handrail, and guard dimensions are missing. Garage separation, sleeping-room escape openings, and pool-barrier hardware are not documented to a level a contractor can build or an inspector can accept. The three areas of greatest concern are the stair geometry, the specification-to-drawing conflicts on the envelope and finishes, and the missing life-safety information at the garage, bedrooms, and guards.
