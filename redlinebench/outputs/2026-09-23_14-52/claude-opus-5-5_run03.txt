# Bonfire House — Construction Document Review

## Step 1: Project Context

- **Project:** Bonfire House, a single-family residence on an unaddressed parcel in Chatham County, NC.
- **Code:** 2018 North Carolina Residential Code, based on the 2015 IRC with amendments.
- **Building and construction type:** R-3 occupancy, Type V-B. Two stories, with a sealed crawl space, attached garage, deck, and above-ground lap pool.
- **Climate zone:** 4A (stated on the documents).
- **Drawing set:**
  - Sheets included: G-series (symbols, code summary, 3D views), site plan, plans, roof plan, elevations, sections, wall and floor assemblies, exterior and deck details, RCP, interior elevations and details, and schedules.
  - Survey (A1.00) removed.
  - Disciplines absent: structural, MEP, and civil.
- **Specifications:** An 11-page narrative outline spec ("Description of Materials, Applications and Allowances"). It is not organized by CSI divisions.

## Summary

The set conveys the design intent well, but it is not yet coordinated enough to bid or build without significant RFIs. Drawings, schedules and specifications disagree on basic items: window manufacturer, wall sheathing and insulation, stud sizes, door materials, finishes and fixture counts.

The three areas of greatest concern are:

1. **Envelope and roofing details.** Snap-lock metal roofing sits on battens at slopes as low as 1/8":12, directly on PT lumber, with no underlayment. E-3 has no WRB. Foundation walls are only parged. Stone and decks are drawn flush with the wood framing.
2. **Life safety.** Garage separation is undefined in both the ceiling and the doors. Safety glazing is not designated. Egress windows cannot be verified. The pool has an open south edge and a 26" stair guard.
3. **Schedules and specs.** Door, window and finish schedules are incomplete and inconsistent, and the narrative spec is full of TBDs, vendor contacts and unresolved owner questions.
