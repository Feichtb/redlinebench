## Summary

This bid set demonstrates competent architectural design with good resolution of many details, particularly at the entry hall's custom wood assemblies and window trim conditions. However, several categories of issues will impede smooth construction:

**Top Three Concerns:**

1. **Fire separation at garage:** The missing 5/8" Type X ceiling specification and absent self-closing hardware note for the garage-to-house door are code violations that will require correction before or during permitting.

2. **Wall assembly layer sequences:** The E-3 wall section appears to show structural sheathing outboard of rigid insulation, which contradicts standard practice and the different configuration shown in E-2/E-4. This needs clarification before framing begins.

3. **Incomplete specifications for bidding:** Extensive "TBD" items across lighting fixtures, finishes, and hardware—combined with discrepancies between specs and schedules (hose bib counts, insulation products, soffit materials)—will generate RFIs and may result in inconsistent bids or change orders.
