## Step 1: Project Context

- **Project / location / code:** Bonfire House, un-addressed parcel, Chatham County, North Carolina. Code basis stated as 2018 NC Residential Code (adopting IRC 2015 w/ amendments); jurisdiction Chatham County Central Permitting. (Note: G0.11 Zoning box also cites IBC Table 503, V-B/R-3 — see E-1.)
- **Building type / construction type:** Single-family dwelling, Occupancy R-3, Construction Type V-B, two stories over a sealed crawl space.
- **Climate zone:** 4A (stated; consistent with central NC Piedmont).
- **Scope of set:** General (G), Architectural (A) only — plans, elevations, building/wall sections, exterior/interior/deck details, RCP, schedules. **Absent disciplines:** Structural (referenced as separate set), Mechanical, Electrical, Plumbing, Civil/Survey (A1.00 survey removed). Pool, well, septic by allowance/others.
- **Specifications:** A narrative "Description of Materials, Applications and Allowances" booklet (11 pages, not CSI-divisioned) is provided.

*Limitations: Survey (A1.00) is removed, so all site grades/elevations are unverifiable and are noted by the spec as "approximate, not based on actual grade." Several window/door tags and small dimension strings are at a resolution where exact counts cannot be fully confirmed; these are flagged as Moderate.*

`A-3 | G0.11 Project Data / Zoning vs A2.01/02 plans | Project described as "four bedroom," but only three rooms are labeled bedrooms (06 Master, 09 Second, 17 Guest); the 4th sleeping room is not identified (affects septic sizing). | High`
`A-4 | A8.01 Room Finish (Rm 11) vs Spec p.5 | Bathroom 11 finish shows tile (T-1) on only the north wall with P-1 elsewhere, but spec specifies Hampton tile on floor and bath walls. | Moderate`

### D. Building envelope and building science

`D-1 | A2.03 roof slope tags vs Spec p.4 (Sheffield SnapLock 450) | Roof areas drawn at 2":12 and 1/4":12 are below the typical minimum slope (≈3":12) for the specified snap-lock standing-seam profile. | High`
`D-2 | A4.00 E-1 / A4.11-5 | Crawl-wall rigid insulation shown as ½" (≈R-2.5) cannot meet the R-10 stem-wall requirement; insulated, sealed crawl space will be under-insulated as drawn. | High`
`D-3 | A4.01 R-1 / A4.11-4 | Roof detailed as an unvented insulated joist assembly, yet the overhang/soffit is called "vented fiberboard soffit"; venting strategy is internally inconsistent and should be coordinated. | Moderate`
`D-4 | A4.01 F-2 vs Spec p.3 (SS-2 limestone at Entry/Laundry/Powder) | Honed limestone over thinset directly on plywood subfloor (framed floor) lacks an uncoupling/cement-board substrate and is deflection-sensitive — risk of cracking. | Moderate`

### E. Code and life safety

`E-1 | G0.11 Zoning box vs Building Code line | Single-family dwelling is reviewed under IRC, yet the Zoning/Construction-type box cites IBC Table 503 for area/height/stories — wrong code path for an R-3 dwelling. | Moderate`
`E-2 | A4.01 F-1 (1/2" gyp) / A7.10-3 vs Level 2 over garage | Garage ceiling/separation shown with ½" gypsum board; habitable rooms over the garage require 5/8" Type X — Type X separation not specified. | High`
`E-3 | A8.01 Door Schedule (garage-to-house door) / A2.01B | No door between Garage 15 and conditioned space is identified as 20-minute / self-closing / self-latching as required for the garage separation. | High`
`E-4 | A5.10/4 South Section, Deck | "26" CABLE RAILING" at deck stairs is below the 36" guard minimum (and outside the 34"–38" handrail range) — non-compliant. | High`
`E-5 | A3.01/A3.02 elevations / window schedule | Large low-sill windows (Types 6, 9) on floors well above exterior grade (crawl/deck side) are not addressed for window fall protection where sill is <24" AFF and >72" above grade. | Moderate`
`E-6 | A8.01 / bedroom plans | Emergency escape & rescue openings are not demonstrated for sleeping rooms; many windows are FIXED, and 2'-0"×2'-0" casements (Type 2C, 4 SF gross) cannot meet the 5.7 SF clear-opening requirement if relied upon. | Moderate`
`E-7 | A5.01 / A5.10 / Spec p.10 | Above-ground pool (4'+ above grade, south edge open, removable deck section) — required pool safety barrier/alarms/gate self-closing-latching not shown/detailed. | Moderate`
`E-8 | Spec p.3 / A5.x | Horizontal cable-rail infill spacing is not specified/dimensioned to demonstrate the 4" sphere limitation. | Moderate`

`F-2 | A4.13/1,2,3 corner windows (reading nook/kitchen/office) | Mulled corner fixed-glass units with mirror returns require a structural corner header/post and specialized flashing; feasibility/structure not resolved on the architectural sheets. | Moderate`

`F-4 | A2.01A note / A7.02 Master Bath | In-floor hydronic radiant ("bidder design layout") is required at Master Bath, but floor assembly F-2 shows no tubing/mortar bed accommodation. | Moderate`
