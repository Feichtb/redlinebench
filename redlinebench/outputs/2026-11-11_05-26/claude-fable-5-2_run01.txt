## Step 1: Project context

- **Project / jurisdiction:** Bonfire House, Chatham County, NC; 2018 North Carolina Residential Code (IRC 2015 with amendments).
- **Building type:** New two-story, four-bedroom single-family house (R-3, Type V-B) on a CMU crawl space, with attached garage, decks and an above-ground lap pool.
- **Climate zone:** 4A (stated on G0.11).
- **Drawing scope:** G-series (symbols, code summary, 3D views) and A-series (site, plans, roof, elevations, sections, assemblies, details, deck/pool, RCP, interior elevations, schedules). The survey sheet is blank, and no structural, MEP, civil or landscape sheets are included.
- **Specifications:** An 11-page narrative outline ("Description of Materials, Applications and Allowances"), not organized by CSI division.
- **Review limitation:** Window tags on the 1/8" and 1/4" sheets are not legible enough to verify counts elevation by elevation. Tag totals read from the plans appear to match the schedule for all types except Type 6, where the plans read one short of the scheduled 8.

## Summary

The set is graphically well developed for a bid set, but the schedules, assembly sheets and narrative spec are not coordinated with each other or with the drawn details. Several building-science and life-safety questions are unresolved, and a large share of selections and scope splits are deferred to "TBD", "verify in field" or "to be coordinated". Bids are therefore likely to vary widely and generate many RFIs.

The three areas of greatest concern are:

1. **Roof assembly and geometry.** Diagonal slopes with seams running across the fall, Galvalume on treated furring, a weather barrier below the insulation, snap-lock panels on 1/4":12 and 2:12 roofs, and roofs draining against taller walls.
2. **Garage separation, glazing and pool safety.** A 1/2" gypsum ceiling under the guest suite, unrated house doors, no safety glazing or egress designations, and an undocumented pool barrier and railings.
3. **Specification and schedule conflicts.** Window manufacturer, wall sheathing and insulation, stud sizes, finish schedule errors, door schedule mismatches, hose bib and plumbing counts, and undefined pool and solar scope.
