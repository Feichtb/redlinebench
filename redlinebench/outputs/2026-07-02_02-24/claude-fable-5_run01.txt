# Construction Document Review — Bonfire House

## Step 1: Project Context

- **Project:** Bonfire House, Bid Set dated 10/20/2021; unaddressed parcel in Chatham County, North Carolina. Jurisdiction: Chatham County Building Inspectors/Central Permitting; **2018 North Carolina Residential Code (IRC 2015 as amended)**.
- **Building type:** New single-family residence (R-3), 4 bedrooms stated, 3,840 SF total / 3,270 SF heated, 2 stories over sealed crawl space, with above-ground lap pool, decks, well, and septic. **Construction Type V-B.**
- **Climate zone:** 4A (stated on G0.11 and consistent with central NC).
- **Drawing set scope:** General (G) and Architectural (A) sheets only — plans, elevations, sections, wall/floor assemblies, exterior/interior details, deck package, RCP, and schedules. Survey sheet intentionally blanked. No structural, MEP, civil, or pool engineering drawings included (referenced but not part of this review).
- **Specifications:** A single narrative "Description of Materials" booklet (11 pages), not organized in CSI divisions.

### D. Building Envelope and Building Science

D-1 | A3.01/A3.02/A6.01 vs. A4.01 R-1 | Elevations and RCP call out "vented fiberboard soffit typical," but R-1 is an unvented compact roof insulated at the roofline — what the soffit vents ventilate (overhang cavity only vs. roof assembly) is undefined and potentially contradictory. | Moderate
D-2 | A4.01 R-1 / G0.11 / spec p.4 | With R-38 cavity batts under R-15 above-deck rigid, the assembly sits exactly at the minimum above-deck ratio for an unvented roof in CZ4A; the unresolved R-49 vs. R-53 discrepancy and any substitution risks condensation at the structural sheathing. | Moderate
D-3 | Spec p.4 vs. A2.03/A3.02/A4.13 | The specified SMI 1.5" SnapLock 450 snap-lock standing seam is applied to roof areas at 2":12, 1/4":12, and 1/8":12, well below the typical 3:12 minimum for snap-lock profiles — the low-slope entry and porch roofs need a mechanically seamed panel or membrane. | High
D-4 | A4.01 F-5 / spec p.6 | The garage is conditioned (split heat pump specified) but the F-5 slab-on-grade shows no perimeter/edge or under-slab insulation. | Moderate
D-5 | Spec p.4 / A2.03 | Rain chains are the sole downspout conveyance for roughly 4,000 SF of 3:12 metal roof; capacity in heavy rain and overflow paths at the gutters are not addressed. | Moderate
D-6 | A4.10 det 4 | The 1/4" mirror panel sealed full-height inside the entry window wall cavity creates a vapor-impermeable, inaccessible layer within the exterior assembly with condensation and serviceability risk. | Moderate

## Summary

This is a well-organized and graphically thorough architectural set for a custom house, but it is not yet coordinated to bid-set standard: the specification and drawings disagree on fundamental scope items (window manufacturer, exterior sheathing/continuous-insulation system, facade cladding assignments, insulation R-values, exterior door materials), and the schedules contain multiple entries that contradict the drawn and specified conditions. The three areas of greatest concern are (1) envelope spec/drawing misalignment — the ZIP-R vs. loose rigid insulation conflict, the double-assigned West facade cladding, and the snap-lock standing seam applied to roofs far below its minimum slope; (2) life-safety gaps — the missing Type X garage ceiling separation below the guest suite, undemonstrated bedroom egress (including the fixed-glass bunk nook), the 26" stair guard, and absent safety-glazing designations; and (3) unresolved scope — pervasive TBDs, blank hardware schedules, and pool/retaining-wall/radiant-floor design deferred without clear responsibility. Some window counts and small plan tags could not be fully verified at the provided resolution and should be re-checked against native files. Resolving the spec-to-drawing conflicts before bid would prevent the largest share of the RFIs and change orders this set would otherwise generate.
