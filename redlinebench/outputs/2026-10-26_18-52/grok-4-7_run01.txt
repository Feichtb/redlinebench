The stated code is the 2018 North Carolina Residential Code (IRC 2015 with amendments); energy compliance is prescriptive Climate Zone 4A. The bid set (10/20/2021, Ben Feicht) is architectural only: general sheets, code summary, 3D views, site plan, plans, elevations, sections, assemblies, details, and schedules. ## Summary

This bid set is graphically developed but not coordinated enough to price or build without a large RFI set. The narrative specification and the drawings describe different products and assemblies for the same elements, and several life-safety dimensions do not close.

The three areas of greatest concern are the exterior wall and roof thermal assemblies (foam inboard of sheathing, ZIP versus plywood, and three different R-values), stair and guard geometry that does not meet residential limits (riser height, headroom, and a 26 in. stair rail), and schedule-to-spec conflicts on windows, door 03-1, cladding exposure, and the powder-room floor, together with missing garage separation and undocumented sleeping-room egress. Grades, the pool retaining wall, and several owner decisions are still open, so bids on those scopes will not be comparable.
