Step 1 – Project context  
• Project: “Bonfire House”, Chatham County NC; 2018 NC Residential Code (IRC 2015 w/-amendments) is the stated jurisdictional basis  
• Occupancy R-3, Construction Type V-B, single-family, two storeys plus crawl space  
• Climate zone 4A (mixed-humid)  
• Drawing set scope: architectural only – general notes, site, plans, enlarged plans, roof, exterior & interior elevations, building & wall sections, assemblies, deck, reflected ceiling, schedules; no structural, MEP, civil, landscape sheets present  
• Specifications: 11-page descriptive booklet (materials/allowances) – not organised by CSI divisions; no formal project manual provided

Summary  
The document set exhibits numerous coordination and completeness gaps. Major discrepancies exist between the brief specification booklet and the drawing schedules (windows, siding, insulation, roofing). Stair geometry, garage separation, and bedroom egress pose clear code compliance issues. Envelope details around roof edges, corner windows, and the flush deck connection are under-detailed and present moisture-control and constructability risks. The three most critical areas are: 1) inconsistent and incomplete life-safety information (stairs, garage door rating, egress windows), 2) conflicting material specifications versus drawings (windows, siding, insulation), and 3) envelope/roof/deck waterproofing details that are insufficient for the mixed-humid climate.
