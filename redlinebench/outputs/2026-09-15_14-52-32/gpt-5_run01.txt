Summary

Overall document quality is good graphically but contains significant coordination gaps between drawings and the narrative specs, plus several critical technical errors. The most serious issues are: (1) envelope/roofing inconsistencies — low roof slopes conflict with the specified standing seam profile and WRB/flashings are not clearly detailed; (2) life-safety code gaps — stair arithmetic, guard heights, and garage separation/door rating are unclear or incorrect; and (3) product and scope mismatches — windows/doors brands, insulation assemblies, and hydronic heating scope are inconsistent between specs and drawings. These will generate RFIs, pricing volatility, and potential rework unless corrected before bidding.
