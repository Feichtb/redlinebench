# STEP 1 — PROJECT CONTEXT

- **Project / location / code:** "Bonfire House," unaddressed parcel, Chatham County, North Carolina. Code cited: 2018 NC Residential Code (IRC 2015 with amendments); jurisdiction Chatham County Central Permitting.
- **Building type / construction type:** New single-family residence, R-3 occupancy, Type V-B per G0.11 (note: construction-type classification and the IBC table cited are not applicable under the Residential Code).
- **Climate zone:** 4A (stated on G0.11; consistent with central NC).
- **Scope of set:** 33 sheets, architectural only — general/code, 3D views, site plan, floor plans (crawl, L1, L1 enlarged N & S, L2, roof), exterior elevations, building/wall sections, wall and horizontal assemblies, exterior details, deck plans/elevations/sections/details, RCP, interior elevations and details, door/window/finish/appliance schedules. **Absent disciplines:** structural, mechanical, plumbing, electrical, civil/grading, landscape. A1.00 Survey is blank ("survey removed for this review").
- **Specifications:** An 11-page narrative "Description of Materials, Applications and Allowances," not organized in CSI divisions and containing no allowance dollar values.

*Review limitation: the drawing set was reviewed as raster images; small text (individual window tags on 1/8" elevations, some dimension strings) is at or below legibility, so element-by-element tag counts between elevations and the window schedule could not be fully verified. Findings below are limited to what could be read with confidence.*

# SUMMARY

This is a well-organized and graphically consistent set for a custom residence, with unusually good assembly documentation (A4.00/A4.01) and a thorough narrative specification — but the two documents were clearly not reconciled with one another, and several systems are documented as design intent rather than as buildable work. The highest-risk area is **specification-to-drawing conflict**: window manufacturer (Marvin vs. Andersen), wall insulation strategy (site-built plywood + foam vs. insulated ZIP sheathing), crawl space insulation (1/2" vs. 2"), ceiling R-value (R-53 vs. R-49), cladding locations (west facade assigned two claddings), and the scope of the PV array (installed vs. "solar ready") will each generate an RFI or change order. The second area of concern is **envelope building science and roofing**: continuous insulation drawn inboard of the structural sheathing, a continuous-insulation layer that is necessarily broken at every wall/roof junction, no roofing underlayment, and a snap-lock standing seam panel specified over roof planes as shallow as 1/8:12. The third is **life-safety documentation**: garage/dwelling separation membrane and door rating, stair handrails/guards/headroom, safety glazing, EERO verification data, pool barrier continuity and gate hardware, and a 26" guard callout are all missing, inconsistent, or non-compliant as shown. Secondary but pervasive issues include schedule errors (missing door 11-1, carpet in the powder room, undefined finish code T-1, no ceiling column, mis-titled interior elevation), an incorrect sheet index entry (A5.11 vs. A5.20), and a large number of unresolved "TBD" selections and unpriced allowances that make the set difficult to bid competitively.
