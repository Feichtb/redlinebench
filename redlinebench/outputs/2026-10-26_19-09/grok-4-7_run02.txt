**Summary**

This is not a complete bid set. The drawings and the narrative specification describe different windows, several different exterior doors and finishes, different insulation products and R-values, and different site and energy scopes, including solar-ready versus a drawn array and a gas furnace versus geothermal still marked TBD. The interior stair is the most immediate life-safety problem: the two sections disagree, and the east section produces risers well above the residential maximum, with guard height, width, and headroom unresolved and a 26" cable rail at the deck stair. Envelope and wet-area assemblies are the third concern: roof drainage sequence and slope conflict, crawl-wall insulation does not meet the stated R-10 sealed-crawl strategy, and shower waterproofing is simply not drawn. A bidder pricing this set would have to qualify the stair, the envelope, the finishes, and a large block of TBD items, or expect a run of RFIs and change orders before foundations.
