## Step 1: Project Context

- **Project**: Bonfire House, Chatham County, North Carolina; 2018 NC Residential Code (adopts IRC 2015 with amendments)
- **Building type**: Single-family residential (R-3 occupancy), Construction Type V-B
- **Climate zone**: 4A (stated in code summary)
- **Drawing set scope**: General sheets (symbols, code summary, 3D views), Architectural sheets (site plan, floor plans, elevations, sections, wall assemblies, details, interior elevations, reflected ceiling plan, schedules). Structural, MEP, and civil drawings are not included.
- **Specifications**: 11-page project specification covering materials, applications, and allowances for all major building systems

## Summary

The Bonfire House bid set demonstrates competent architectural documentation with thoughtful details for custom elements like the entry hall wood paneling and exterior cladding systems. However, the documents contain several significant coordination failures that would generate RFIs and potential change orders during construction.

**Top three areas of concern:**

1. **Specification-to-drawing conflicts**: The window manufacturer discrepancy (Marvin on drawings vs. Andersen in specs) and wall insulation mismatches (thickness, stud depth, and R-value claims) would immediately surface during submittal review and require resolution before procurement.

2. **Code compliance gaps**: The bedroom emergency egress situation — particularly Second Bedroom (09) appearing to rely on fixed windows — and the incomplete garage-to-dwelling separation documentation (fire rating, self-closing hardware) require verification and potential design revision.

3. **Thermal envelope inconsistencies**: The crawl space insulation as detailed (1/2" rigid showing R-2 to R-3) cannot achieve the claimed R-10 value, and the wall cavity/insulation mismatch in assembly E-2 indicates the energy calculations may not align with constructible assemblies.
